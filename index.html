About Me

Pharmaceutical coating and production professional with 11+ years on the line at Renata PLC, Rajendrapur Potent Product Facility. Started as an industrial apprentice in 2014 and grew into Junior Officer – Coating Area through consistent, hands-on work on sugar coating, film coating, and solid-dosage operations.

Experience in a WHO-approved potent product manufacturing facility with multi-regulatory compliance exposure. Focused on coating machine operation, strict GMP discipline, and efficient, high-quality solid dosage production.

From apprenticeship through production operation and coating-area supervision, the work has stayed close to the process: prepare well, operate carefully, document clearly, and help the next shift succeed.
